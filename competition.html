Workshop Competition

Fish Species Recognition Challenge

Build a classifier that identifies fish species from images, including species seen during training and unseen species in the test set.

June 1, 2026 - September 1, 2026

Go to Codabench

Overview

Image-based fish species recognition

Participants will train models on labelled fish images and submit predictions for unlabelled test images. The test set contains both species seen during training and species not present in the training split.

The dataset includes class descriptions for all seen and unseen classes, allowing participants to explore visual, textual, and multimodal approaches.

Details

Competition information

Task description

Build models for fish species classification using labelled training images and class descriptions.

Open-set recognition

Systems will be evaluated on known species as well as unseen species that do not appear in the training set.

Evaluation

Final rankings are based on overall classification accuracy. Codabench also reports accuracy separately for seen and unseen classes.

Participation

Register and submit through the Codabench competition page. Registration is auto-approved.
