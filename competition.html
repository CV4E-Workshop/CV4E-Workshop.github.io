Workshop Competition

FishNet Challenge

A text-guided open-set fine-grained fish classification benchmark for multimodal species recognition under naturally long-tailed distributions.

Launch May 31, 2026 · Final submissions August 31, 2026

Overview

Text-guided open-set fine-grained fish classification

FishNet advances multimodal species recognition for marine ecology. Participants will develop models that integrate visual data and expert text descriptions to classify marine species.

Models are trained on a set of seen classes with textual metadata and evaluated on both held-out instances of seen classes and entirely novel unseen species.

Details

Competition information

Task description

Build models for fine-grained marine species classification using images and expert text descriptions.

Open-set recognition

Systems will be evaluated on known species as well as novel unseen species that do not appear in the training set.

Evaluation

Submissions are ranked using a unified metric that balances in-set and out-of-set detection, fine-grained accuracy on known species, and zero-shot generalization to unseen species.
